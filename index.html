05 / What's Next

A Guinness World Records cook-a-thon attempt.

Founder · Personal Chef

Rita Mensah

Known to her kitchen as Chef Rheetah.

Rita Oluwaseun Mensah is the founder of Rheetah's Kitchen, a private chef and catering practice in Lagos. She graduated in Finance from Ekiti State University, worked her way through hospitality and sales, and in 2021 turned her passion for cooking into a business.

She is professionally trained, having completed the culinary program led by Hilda Baci — the current Guinness World Record holder for the longest cooking marathon. That lineage matters. Rita cooks with the same discipline: pineapple boat pasta, premium seafood okra, date boxes, single-serve catering for events. In 2026 she was selected as one of 204 vendors for the GTCO Food & Drink Festival, and sold out three days running.

What is next is bigger. Rita is preparing her own Guinness World Records cook-a-thon attempt. Days on her feet, one kitchen, food shared with attendees and donated to charity partners across Lagos. Brand and media partners are welcome.
